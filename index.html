Steklov–Pólya–Szegő conjecture

We prove the polygonal Weinstock-type conjecture for every integer N ≥ 3. At fixed perimeter, the regular N-gon uniquely maximizes the first positive Steklov eigenvalue among simple planar polygons with at most N sides.

Main theorem

Let Ω be a simple planar polygon of positive area, with perimeter P(Ω) and at most N sides. Let σ₁(Ω) be its first positive Steklov eigenvalue and let RN be a regular N-gon. Then

P(Ω) σ₁(Ω) ≤ P(RN) σ₁(RN).

Equality holds exactly when Ω is similar to RN. The theorem covers convex and nonconvex polygons. Each maximal straight segment of the boundary counts as one side.

Papers

  • N = 3
    The sharp Steklov inequality for triangles and its linear stability (PDF)

    The equilateral triangle is the unique maximizer up to similarity. The proof also gives two-sided linear stability.

  • N = 4
    A sharp Steklov inequality for quadrilaterals by interpolation along a diagonal (PDF)

    The square is the unique maximizer up to similarity. The proof treats both convex and concave quadrilaterals.

  • N ≥ 5
    The first Steklov eigenvalue of simple polygons with a prescribed upper bound on the number of sides (PDF)

    Theorem 6.1 gives the sharp inequality and its equality case for every N ≥ 5, including pentagons and hexagons.
