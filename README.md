# Steklov Polygon Inequalities

Research manuscripts on sharp Steklov eigenvalue inequalities, rigidity, and quantitative stability for polygons.

## Papers

### N = 3: Triangles

**The Sharp Steklov Inequality for Triangles and Its Linear Stability**  
**Authors:** Zhuo Cheng and Yi'an Zhang

[Read PDF](N3_Triangle.pdf) | [LaTeX source](N3_Triangle.tex)

For every nondegenerate triangle $T$, with $E$ an equilateral triangle of unit side length, the manuscript establishes

$$
P(T)\,\sigma_1(T)\le 3\sigma_1(E),
$$

with equality precisely for equilateral triangles. It also gives a two-sided linear stability inequality

$$
c\,d(T)\le 1-\frac{P(T)\sigma_1(T)}{3\sigma_1(E)}\le C\,d(T),
\qquad d(T)=\frac{3\ell_{\max}(T)}{P(T)}-1.
$$

The approach combines affine transplantation, boundary moment identities, and Rellich identities.

### N = 4: Quadrilaterals

**A Sharp Steklov Inequality for Quadrilaterals by Interpolation Along a Diagonal**  
**Authors:** Zhuo Cheng and Yi'an Zhang

[Read PDF](N4_Quadrilateral.pdf) | [LaTeX source](N4_Quadrilateral.tex)

For every simple nondegenerate quadrilateral $Q$, convex or nonconvex, the manuscript proves
$
P(Q)\,\sigma_1(Q)\le 8\tau\tanh\tau,
\qquad \tan\tau\,\tanh\tau=1,\quad 0<\tau<\pi/2,
$
with equality only for squares. For convex quadrilaterals with four genuine corners, it also gives quantitative stability: the deficit controls affine distortion linearly and displacement of the diagonal midpoints quadratically near the square.

The proof is purely analytic. In the convex case, a piecewise-affine map across a diagonal transports trial functions that interpolate between the square's first Steklov eigenfunctions and the coordinate functions. In the nonconvex case, a boundary-variance estimate gives a strict bound below the square's value.
